\caption{Chance-normalised accuracy of all $17$ systems on the $15$ curriculum subjects with at
least $150$ labelled items. CFA and FRM are drawn as separate panels because the two examinations
share no subject and use different option counts, and Acc$^{*}$ removes that difference by
normalising each item against its own chance rate. Column headers give the item count, and rows are
grouped by access mode then ordered by mean Acc$^{*}$. Two readings the aggregate leaderboard
hides. First, ordering is not preserved across subjects: GPT-OSS-120B leads GPT-4o on thirteen of
the fifteen but trails it on Ethical and Professional Standards and on Financial Statement
Analysis. Second, the spread between best and worst system is itself subject-dependent, $20.5$
points on Quantitative Analysis against $49.0$ on Ethical and Professional Standards, where the
frontier systems exceed $89$ while the finance-specialized models sit in the twenties and forties.
Subject labels exist only for the held-out half, so the figure describes that partition and covers
$4{,}217$ items.}
