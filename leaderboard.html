FinExam-10K Leaderboard

10,198 CFA and FRM aligned examination items spanning all five programme stages under one evaluation protocol. Seventeen frozen systems, one pass each, temperature 0. Items derive from CFA and FRM aligned preparatory materials available for academic research. Official CFA Institute and GARP examination content is excluded, and neither body is affiliated with or endorses this work.

Four views of the same seventeen systems. Ranking on the full corpus flatters everyone. Restrict to items that are hard and answerable from their own text, and fourteen of seventeen fall below chance. Restrict once more and the ranking stops existing.

What the four views are. Full corpus is every item. Context-complete drops the items whose stem refers to a table or shared vignette that was an image in the source and was therefore not extracted, so a failure there cannot be blamed on absent background. Hard and context-complete is the intersection of that set with the hard band, and it is the cleanest difficulty evidence the benchmark has. Zero-solve is the set no system in the panel answered correctly.

Difficulty. Bands come from the consensus of these same seventeen systems, balanced across the two access modes so that the ten API-served and seven locally-served systems weigh equally. Thresholds are two thirds and one third. A band is a property of the item, so restricting to any subset never moves an item between bands.

Protocol. One pass per system, temperature 0, top-p 1, seed 202607, 32,768 token context, 8,192 token completion limit. Reasoning effort differs across systems where the provider exposes it, so the ranking is not an equal-conditions capability comparison. Responses from which no option letter could be parsed are scored as incorrect.

Splits. 5,110 mock and practice items are released. 5,088 real-examination items are held out and not distributed. Both halves follow the same filtering, reannotation and scoring procedure. Author, institution and repository identifiers withheld for review.
